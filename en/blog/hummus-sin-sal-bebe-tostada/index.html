Salt-free hummus for babies, with toast strips

By Martina Solés · 6 November 2026 · 3 min read

Pulses, iron and their first taste of sesame, in a texture that's perfect for starting out. Spread on toast, they can pick it up by themselves.

From 6 monthsPrep 10 minNo cooking4-5 servings

Allergens: Pulses (chickpeas)SesameGluten (bread)

Ingredients

  • 200 g cooked chickpeas, no added salt (if tinned, rinse them really well)
  • 1 tablespoon tahini (sesame paste)
  • A squeeze of lemon juice
  • 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
  • Water, as needed
  • Salt-free bread

Step by step

  1. 1

    Blend the chickpeas with the tahini, lemon and oil.

  2. 2

    Add water little by little until you have a smooth paste with no hard lumps.

  3. 3

    Toast the bread and cut it into finger-sized strips.

  4. 4

    Spread a thin layer of hummus over the strips.

How to serve it by age

6-9 monthsstrips of toast with a thin layer of hummus. They can also dip sticks of cooked vegetables.
From 9 monthssmall pieces of toast, or hummus with chunks of cooked vegetables.

Storage

Hummus keeps in the fridge, covered, for 1 day. It can be frozen in small portions for up to 1 month.

No added salt or sugar. Before serving, check the temperature and texture are right: it should squash easily between your fingers.

FAQ

Can I use shop-bought hummus?

Best not. Shop-bought hummus usually contains quite a lot of salt. Homemade with unsalted chickpeas is just as quick.

What if they haven't tried sesame yet?

Tahini is sesame, one of the main allergens. Offer it for the first time at home, in the morning and in a small amount, and if it goes well you can give it as normal.

Can I make it without tahini?

Yes. Swap it for a little more olive oil. It's just as tasty, though without the sesame flavour.
