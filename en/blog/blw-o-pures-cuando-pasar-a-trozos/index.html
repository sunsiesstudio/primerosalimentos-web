BLW or purées: the differences and when to move to finger food

By Martina Solés · 22 November 2026 · 5 min read

Purée, BLW or a bit of both. The short answer: no baby-led, responsive method is better than another. What does matter is not staying on smooth purée for too long.

The three ways to start

  • Blended and spoon-fed. Purée that gradually gets lumpier. You feed it with a spoon.
  • Baby-led weaning (BLW). Soft pieces, not blended, right from the start. They pick them up, bring them to their mouth and decide how much they eat.
  • Mixed. Some purée at some meals and finger food at others.

Which is best?

According to the recommendations of the Spanish Paediatric Association (AEP), no responsive method is better than another. Choose the one that suits your family and your baby. What matters with any of them: respecting their hunger and fullness cues, and offering variety.

Baby-led weaning isn't recommended if there are neurological or motor difficulties, or if the baby isn't gaining weight. In those cases, talk to your health visitor or GP first.

If you start with purée: when to move to finger food

The key: lumps as soon as possible, never later than 8 or 9 months. Introducing lumps after 9 months is linked to more feeding problems and to eating less fruit and veg.

  1. Around 6 months: thick purée, not runny.
  2. Soon after: mashed with a fork, with lumps.
  3. 8-9 months: lumps and soft strips to pick up by hand.
  4. Around 9 months: once they use a pincer grip, small soft pieces the size of a chickpea.
  5. Around 12 months: eats pretty much like the rest of the family, with the texture adapted.

The easy texture test

If you can squash it between your thumb and index finger, or with your tongue against the roof of your mouth, the texture is right. Strips should be finger-sized (5-6 cm), so they can hold them in their fist with a bit sticking out.

FAQ

Is baby-led weaning more dangerous than purée?

When it's done safely (soft foods, the right shape, and the baby sitting up and supervised), studies haven't found more choking than with purées.

Can I mix purée and finger food?

Yes, the mixed approach is perfectly valid.

How long can they keep eating smooth purée?

It's best not to carry on with smooth purée beyond 8 or 9 months. Add lumps little by little.
